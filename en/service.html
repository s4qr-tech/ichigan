Common challenges

New businesses that won't start, won't move, or can't be decided. Are you carrying this alone?

  • The person in charge is also running the existing business and has no time for research or hypotheses
  • You want an objective view of market size and competition before submitting a budget proposal
  • There are several business ideas and you can't decide which to validate first
  • Past research or outsourcing ended with a report and didn't lead to a next move
  • The idea is too small for consultants, and the cost and timeline don't fit
  • You want your young employees to gain management and hands-on experience in new business

For each project, ICHIGAN formsa time-limited business validation team to resolve these challenges small and fast.

The full picture

Discover, define, build, validate. You choose how many of the four steps we take on.

PHASE 01｜DISCOVER

Research

Desk research and interviews with the people involved clarify the business environment and facts. We grasp market size, growth and room to enter from primary sources, and compare existing players' value and pricing.

Deliverables: market research report / competitor analysis

PHASE 02｜DEFINE

Form hypotheses

We identify bottlenecks from the facts and structure them as questions to validate, defining how target customers map to the value offered.

Deliverables: hypotheses and key issues

PHASE 03｜BUILD

Design

We create a design for validating hypotheses at minimum cost. Metrics and decision criteria are agreed before execution, and team, timeline and effort are broken down so your organization can run it.

Deliverables: PoC design document

PHASE 04｜VALIDATE

Validate

We compare results against the criteria set in advance and present continue, change or stop as options backed by evidence. Handover materials are included.

Deliverables: validation results, improvement proposals, handover materials

From consultation to delivery

Your team joins three times — kickoff, mid-term review and delivery — about 4 hours in total.

  1. Free consultation

    In about 30 minutes online, we hear about the business you're considering and where it's stuck. The theme doesn't need to be fixed.

  2. Proposal and estimate

    We organize scope, timeline and team, and present a plan with reference pricing.

  3. Kickoff

    We align on the purpose, decision criteria and form of the deliverables. These criteria become the yardstick for the final decision.

  4. Research, validation and mid-term review

    ICHIGAN and the young team carry out research, analysis and materials, sharing progress to confirm direction.

  5. Delivery and handover

    We hand over the deliverables and decision material, plus handover documents so your team can take the next step.

Sample deliverable

A sample structure of the market research report delivered on the Scan plan. Items are adjusted to the theme, but we always write down what to decide next.

  1. Executive summary

    The research conclusion and the next questions to decide, on a single page.

  2. Market overview

    Market size, growth and key players, organized from primary sources and public data.

  3. Customers and problems

    From interviews, a concrete account of who struggles and in what situations.

  4. Competitors' value and pricing

    Existing services compared on the same axes, showing where there is room to enter.

  5. Questions for the next validation

    What to check next to decide whether to continue, change or stop, plus a list of sources.

FAQ

Can we consult you before we've decided whether to go ahead?

Yes. You can consult us while the theme is still open, or when you need to prioritize between several candidates. In a free consultation we'll explain how validation would proceed and give a rough estimate.

How much can we hand over?

You can choose the scope from the four steps: discover, define, build and validate. You can also start with research only, on the Scan plan.

How much of our team's time does it take?

About 4 hours in total, across the kickoff, mid-term review and delivery. Everything is done online.

Who holds the contract and the responsibility?

The contract is with ICHIGAN only, and ICHIGAN is responsible for the quality of the deliverables.

Can our own employees join the team?

Yes. If you wish, your challengers can join the validation team, gaining management and hands-on experience while moving the new business forward.

Who is the young team?

Young members managed by ICHIGAN who carry out research, analysis and materials. ICHIGAN owns design, project management and quality.

How long does it take?

On the Scan plan, 2–3 weeks as standard. If the scope grows, we design it individually.

Are there any restrictions on industry or region?

None. Everything is done online, so you can work with us regardless of industry or location.
