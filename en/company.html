About ICHIGAN

Company

ICHIGAN takes on new-business validation as an outside team, and turns that field into a place where young people and company challengers grow through real work.

MESSAGE

A cycle in which each challenge
leads to the next.

What building a business truly requires is not simply gathering highly capable people. I believe it is building relationships in which people tell each other the truth, trust one another, and take on challenges facing the same direction.

I, too, have wanted to take on industries that interested me, yet could not see “what kind of work exists” or “how to get in,” and was unable to take the first step.

That is why I want to create an environment where young people come to know industries and companies deeply through real work, and gain experience that leads to their future. And for companies, I wanted to make it possible not only to hire and then train, but to welcome people who already understand the industry and the company.

Rather than judging potential by age or experience alone, we face each person's will and ability and build businesses together on a foundation of trust. Young people and companies grow together, and each challenge leads to the next. ICHIGAN creates those relationships and that cycle from the front line of business development.

Soh Daike, Founder, ICHIGAN
