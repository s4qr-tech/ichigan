WHY ICHIGAN

New businesses stall not because of
ability, but because of “structure.”

Researching markets, forming hypotheses and designing validation while running the existing business.
Few companies can do all that on the side.

The idea is still too small for a consulting firm, and one-off outsourcing leaves only a report.
Meanwhile, the questions that need validating keep piling up.

For each project, ICHIGAN forms a time-limited business validation team, and disbands it when the work is done.
What remains is the material your team needs to take the next step.

FAQ

FAQ about new business validation

Can we consult you before we've decided whether to go ahead?

Yes. You can consult us while the theme is still open, or when you need to prioritize between several candidates. In a free consultation we'll explain how validation would proceed and give a rough estimate.

How much can we hand over?

You can choose the scope from the four steps: discover, define, build and validate. You can also start with research only, on the Scan plan.

How much of our team's time does it take?

About 4 hours in total, across the kickoff, mid-term review and delivery. Everything is done online.

Who holds the contract and the responsibility?

The contract is with ICHIGAN only, and ICHIGAN is responsible for the quality of the deliverables.
