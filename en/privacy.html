Privacy Policy

ICHIGAN (“we”) handles personal information received through our inquiry forms as follows.

1. Business information

Name: ICHIGAN
Representative: Soh Daike
Contact: soh-daike@yahoo.ne.jp

2. Information we collect

For company inquiries, we collect company name, department, name, email address, plan of interest and inquiry details.

For inquiries from young people, we collect name, age, affiliation, email address, field of interest, motivation, portfolio URL, and, for those aged 16–17, whether parental consent has been obtained.

3. Purpose of use

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

  • Responding to inquiries, scheduling interviews and contacting you
  • Proposing services, and concluding and performing contracts
  • Information about joining projects, and evaluating participants’ work
  • Information about career opportunities, with your consent
  • Statistical analysis to improve our services (in a form that does not identify individuals)

4. Provision to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties except with your consent or as required by law. If sharing is necessary to carry out a project, we will obtain your consent in advance and share only what is needed.

5. Management

Information is managed on cloud services provided by Google, and access is limited to those who need it for their work. Information past the period needed to achieve its purpose is deleted appropriately.

6. Eligible age and minors

Inquiries (applications) from young people are open to those aged 16 to 30 at the time of applying. Age is determined as of the time of application.

Applicants aged 16 or 17 should apply after obtaining parental consent. The form asks whether parental consent has been obtained.

7. Requests for disclosure, correction or deletion

If you wish to request disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion of your personal information, please contact soh-daike@yahoo.ne.jp . We will respond promptly after verifying your identity.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as needed. Any revisions will be posted on this page.
